# AI Support Copilot Pilot

**Client**: Prasanna (CIO / Business Lead / Product Owner)
**POD**: Gyde AI POD
**Duration**: 4 weeks (2 sprints)
**Status**: Discovery

---

## Engagement Summary

Build an AI support copilot that helps agents understand incoming tickets, retrieve relevant knowledge, draft grounded responses, recommend next-best-action (including escalation), and provide traceability for review.

Phase 1 scope: one support queue, one knowledge source, one escalation mechanism, human-in-the-loop only.
